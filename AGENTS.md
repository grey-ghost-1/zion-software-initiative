# Zion — Assistant Instructions

## Project scope

- Zion's planned flagships are Harbor and GreenHub only.
- Harbor is planned community-resource coordination, starting with a proposed
  Roanoke, Virginia pilot for food banks, shelters, and veteran services.
- GreenHub is planned coordination of Roanoke-area environmental efforts, such as
  cleanups and trail days, volunteer sign-ups, and an impact counter. It is
  intended as the first implementation of Harbor's coordination model.
- Both initiatives are planned, not live. Do not claim users, partners, or
  outcomes that have not been verified.

## Safety and data

- Use synthetic or curated public data only; do not add real resident, patient,
  shelter-client, volunteer, or partner data.
- Preserve data provenance, organization boundaries, role-based access, and audit
  trails for administrative actions.
- Sensitive actions require human approval. Do not add automated dispatch,
  diagnosis, medical or legal advice, or emergency response.
- Never commit credentials, personal data, or local environment files.

## Development

- Follow the existing monorepo structure and project conventions.
- Ask before adding dependencies, changing the stack, or provisioning paid
  services.
- Keep changes focused, test them with the repository's established checks, and
  do not report a feature as live unless it is deployed and verified.
