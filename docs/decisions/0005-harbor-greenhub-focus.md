# ADR 0005: Focus Zion on Harbor and GreenHub

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-20

## Context

The initial initiative list was broader than the project can responsibly build
and validate. Zion needs a clear, limited focus and must not imply that proposed
services or community partnerships already exist.

## Decision

Zion will focus on two planned initiatives:

- **Harbor:** community-resource coordination, beginning with a proposed Roanoke,
  Virginia pilot for food banks, shelters, and veteran services.
- **GreenHub (Roanoke Green Hub):** coordination of Roanoke-area environmental
  efforts such as cleanups and trail days, with volunteer sign-ups and an impact
  counter. It is intended as the first implementation of Harbor's coordination
  model.

Both initiatives are planned, not live. No users, partners, or outcomes are
claimed. Development and demonstrations continue to use synthetic examples and
curated public data only. Existing safety, privacy, provenance, and human-review
boundaries remain unchanged.

## Consequences

Public descriptions and future architecture work will focus on Harbor and
GreenHub. The earlier initiative list remains visible only as historical context
in prior decision records; it does not describe current project scope.
