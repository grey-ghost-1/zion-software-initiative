# ADR 0002: Use synthetic and curated public data

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

## Context

The proposed domains may involve people in vulnerable situations and health or
shelter information. This foundation has no consent, governance, or operational
need for real records.

## Decision

Use synthetic examples and curated public datasets only. Record provenance and
license before adding public datasets. Prohibit personal, patient, shelter-client,
partner, and other sensitive records.

## Consequences

Demonstrations cannot represent real outcomes. Tests and examples stay safe to
publish. Any later change requires a new threat assessment and decision record.
